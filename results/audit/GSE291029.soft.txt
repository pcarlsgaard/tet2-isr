!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = RPF: Cells were collected by trypsinization (TrypLE, Gibco), counted, washed in PBS0, and approximately 2000 were resuspended in 2.5 µL polysome lysis buffer [20 mM Tris pH 7.5, 15 mM MgCl2, 5 mM CaCl2, 150 mM NaCl, 1% Triton X-100, 2 U/µL RNaseIN Plus (Promega), 0.1 mM Cycloheximide (Sigma Aldrich), 0.1 mM Chloramphenicol (Sigma Aldrich)] and incubated on ice. !Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = RPF: To generate ribosome-protected footprints, 2.5 µL of Micrococcal Nuclease (MNase, 62.5 U/µL, New England Biolabs) was added to each sample and incubated at 37 °C for 30 minutes. In order to stop digestion, 2.5 µL of stop mix [0.0186 U/µL Thermolabile Proteinase K (New England Biolabs), 62 mM EGTA (Sigma Aldrich), 16.5 mM EDTA (Ambion), and 697.5 mM guanidium thiocyanite (GuSCN, Sigma Aldrich)] was added to each well, and samples were incubated at 37 °C for 30 minutes then 55 °C for 10 minutes and held at 4 °C.  After ribosome footprint digestion, libraries were constructed using a one-pot small-RNA library preparation protocol that incorporated end repair, two RNA ligations, cDNA synthesis, and an indexing PCR. First, 2.5 µL of end-repair mix [4.1× of 10× T4 RNA Ligase Buffer (New England Biolabs), 16.4 mM MgCl2, 4.1 mM uridine triphosphate (New England Biolabs), 1.37 U/µL T4  Polynucleotide Kinase (New England Biolabs), and 0.82 U/µL RNaseIN Plus] was added to each well, and plates were incubated at 37 °C for 1 hour and held at 4 °C. Next, 13.2 µL of 3’ ligation brew [1× T4 RNA Ligase Buffer (New England Biolabs), 1 µM pre-adenylated 3’ adapter (OMV630 Integrated DNA Technologies), 35.5 % PEG-8000 (New England Biolabs), 0.1 % Tween-20 (Sigma Aldrich), 1 U/µL RNaseIN Plus, and 21.3 U/µL T4 RNA Ligase 2 Truncated KQ (New England Biolabs)] was added to each well and plates were incubated at 4 °C for 18 hours. The cDNA synthesis primer was then pre-annealed to the 3’ ligation products by adding 2.5 µL of the RT primer mix [5.2 µM RT primer (OMV572, Integrated DNA Technologies), 13.5 µM adenosine triphosphate (ATP, New England Biolabs), and 1 % Tween-20] to each well, heating to 65 °C for 1 minute, 37 °C for 2 minutes, 25 °C for 2 minutes, and holding at 4 °C. Five-prime adapters were then ligated by adding 7.8 µL of 5’ ligation brew [1× T4 RNA Ligase Buffer, 30.75 % PEG-8000, 0.1 % Tween-20, 0.5 µM 5’ adapter (OMV632, Integrated DNA Technologies), 1.25 U/µL T4 RNA Ligase 1 (Ambion)] and incubating at 37 °C for 2 hours and holding at 4 °C. Complementary DNA synthesis was then peformed by adding 38.5 µL of reverse transcription brew [1.88× 5× RT Buffer (ThermoFisher Scientific), 1.25 mM dNTPs (Promega), 0.1875 % Tween-20, 1.875 U/µL RNaseIN Plus, and 9.375 U/µL Maxima H Minus Reverse Transcriptase (ThermoFisher Scientific)] to each well, and heating at 50 °C for 1 hour, then 85 °C for 5 minutes and holding at 4 °C. Finally, libraries were indexed during PCR by first transferring 3.6 µL of 20 µM unique forward index primers (Integrated DNA Technologies) and 78 µL of PCR brew [1.5× Q5 Hot Start High-Fidelity 2× Master Mix (New England Biolabs), 0.15 % Tween-20, and 0.94 µM reverse index primer (Integrated DNA Technologies)] to half of the cDNA from each sample. Plates were then incubated at 98 °C for 30 s followed by 8 cycles of 98 °C for 15 s, 65 °C 30 s, 72 °C for 30 s, and then a final incubation at 72 °C for 5 min and holding at 4 °C. Plates were then frozen at -20 °C until gel purification.  After library construction the samples were purified. Product was cleaned up using AMPure XP beads (Beckman Coulter) that had been diluted 6× in bead binding buffer [20 % PEG-8000 (Sigma Aldrich) 2.5 M Sodium Chloride (Sigma Aldrich)]; diluted beads were added to the sample at a 2.1:1 ratio, and the final product was resuspended in 20 µL of low TE buffer [LoTET, 3 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0 (Ambion), 0.2 mM EDTA pH 8.0 (Gibco), 0.1 % Tween-20]. Half of each of the cleaned-up library pools was then run on a 20 cm 7 % polyacrylamide gel at 300 V until the lower marker ran off of the gel. The ~10 base-pair region from 175-185, corresponding to an insert size of ~30-40 nt was excised. The band was then crushed and soaked overnight at 4 °C in LTET8. Finally, eluate was cleaned up using AMPure XP beads that had been diluted 6× in bead binding buffer and resuspended in LTET8.  VASA: Cells were first lysed by adding 2.5 µL of Lysis and Fragmentation brew [3.4× First Strand Buffer (Invitrogen), 0.024 U/µL Thermolabile Proteinase K (New England Biolabs), 0.2 % Triton X-100, 1:125000 diluted ERCC Spike-in] and incubating at 25 °C for 1 hour, then 55 °C for 10 minutes and holding at 4 °C. Next, 2.5  µL of End Repair and Poly(A) tailing brew was added [0.6× First Strand Buffer (Invitrogen), 20 mM DTT (Invitrogen), 7.5 µM ATP (New England Biolabs), 0.075 U/µL E. coli Poly(A) Polymerase (New England Biolabs), 1 U/µL T4 Polynucleotide Kinase (New England Biolabs), 4 U/µL RNase OUT (Invitrogen)] and incubated at 37 °C for 1 hour and held at 4 °C. Then 2.5  µL of Reverse Transcription Brew was added [2 mM each dNTP (Promega), 16 U/µL SuperScript III (Invitrogen)] and incubated at 50 °C for 1 hour and held at 4 °C. Finally, 55 µL of Second Strand brew [1.14× Second Strand Buffer (Invitrogen), 0.23 mM each dNTP (Promega), 0.32 U/µL E. coli DNA Polymerase I (Invitrogen), 0.018 U/µL RNase H (Invitrogen)] was added and samples were incubated at 16 °C, 85 °C for 20 minutes, and held at 4 °C. Double stranded libraries were cleaned up using AMPure XP beads (Beckman) that had been diluted 8× in Bead Binding Buffer [20 % PEG-8000 (Sigma Aldrich), 2.5 M NaCl (Ambion), 0.02 % Sodium Azide (Sigma Aldrich)], resuspended in 12 µL of water, and amplified using in vitro transcription at 37 °C overnight using the MegaScript T7 kit (Invitrogen) in a 30 µL reaction.  In vitro transcription was stopped by adding 12 µL of ExoSAP-IT (Invitrogen) and incubating at 37 °C  for 15 minutes and holding at 4 °C. Resulting amplified RNA (aRNA) was then cleaned up using AMPure XP Beads at a 1.8× ratio and resuspended in 10 µL of water. 600 ng of aRNA was then mixed with 10 µL hybridization brew [100 mM Tris pH 7.5, 200 mM NaCl (Ambion), 5 µM rRNA depletion oligonucleotides], incubated at 95 °C for 2 minutes, then decreased to 45 °C at a rate of 0.1 °C/s. While keeping samples at 45 °C, 10 µL of RNase H mix [100 mM Tris pH 7.5, 200 mM NaCl (Ambion), 40 mM MgCl2, 1 U/µL Hybridase Thermostable RNase H (Epicenter)] was added, mixed by pipetting, and incubated at 45 °C for 30 minutes and held at 4 °C. Next, 30 µL of DNase mix [1.67 mM CaCl2, 0.133 U/µL RQ1 DNase (Promega)] was added and samples were incubated at 37 °C for 30 minutes. The resulting rRNA-depleted samples were cleaned up using AMPure XP beads at a 1.6× ratio and eluted in 5 µL of water.  The cleaned up, rRNA-depleted samples were then each mixed with 1 µL of RA3 adapter (/5rApp/TGGAATTCTCGGGTGCCAAGG/3ddC/, 20 µM), denatured at 70 °C for 2 minutes, then snap chilled on ice. 4 µL of ligation brew was added [2.5× T4 RNA Ligase Reaction Buffer (New England Biolabs), 50 U/µL T4 RNA Ligase 2 truncated (New England Biolabs), 10 U/µL RNaseOUT (Invitrogen), 12.5% PEG-8000 (New England Biolabs)], and incubated at 25 °C for 1 hour and held at 4 °C. Ligated RNA was then converted to cDNA by adding 3 µL of dNTP-primer mix [3.33 µM each dNTPs (Promega), 13.33 µM RTP (CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGA)], incubating at 65 °C for 5 minutes and snap chilling on ice. 8 µL of RT brew [2.5× First Strand Buffer (Invitrogen), 12.5 mM DTT (Invitrogen), 5 U/µL RNaseOUT (Invitrogen), 25 U/µL SuperScript III (Invitrogen)], was added, incubated at 50 °C for 1 hour, 70 °C for 15 minutes, and held at 4 °C. RNA was next degraded by adding 1 µL RNase A (10 mg/mL, ThermoFisher Scientific) and incubating at 37 °C for 30 minutes. Complementary DNA was cleaned up using AMPure XP beads at a 1× ratio and resuspended in 10 µL of water. Purified cDNA was amplified by adding PCR brew [1.25 × NEBNext PCR mix (New England Biolabs), 0.5 µM RP1 (AATGATACGGCGACCACCGAGATCTACACGTTCAGAGTTCTACAGTCCGA), and 0.5 µM indexed RPI (CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGAT[6 bases]GTGACTGGAGTTCCTTGGCACCCGAGAATTCCA)], and incubating at 98 °C for 30 s followed by 8 cycles of 98 °C for 15 s, 60 °C 30 s, 72 °C for 30 s, and then a final incubation at 72 °C for 5 min and holding at 4 °C. Amplified and indexed libraries were cleaned up with two successive AMPure XP bead cleanups with 0.8× ratio and resuspended in LTET8.

!Sample_data_processing = See github repository https://github.com/mvanins/scRiboSeq_manuscript/tree/main/data_processing for code associated with raw data processing. See github repository https://github.com/mvanins/WEE1_manuscript for scripts used to create associated figures.  RPF: Basecalls were performed on the Illumina NextSeq 2000. Reads from each plate were demultiplexed using bcl2fastq (version 2.20.0.422) with --use-bases-mask Y*,I*,Y* --no-lane-splitting --mask-short-adapter-reads 0 --minimum-trimmed-read-length 0. Read 1 contains the insert and Read 2 contains the cell barcode. Next, the UMI was extracted from the first 10 bases of read 1 and concatenated to the start of the cell barcode. Adapter sequences were then trimmed from read 1 using cutadapt (version 2.7) with -m 15: -a TGGAATTCTCGGGT. Trimmed reads were aligned to the reference genome using STARSolo (version 2.7.3a_2020-05-22) with a 50-base overhang (--sjdbOverhang 50) with the following parameters: --seedSearchStartLmax 10 –alignIntronMax 1000000 --outFilterScoreMin 0 --outFilterMultimapNmax 1 --chimScoreSeparation 10 --chimScoreMin 20 --chimSegmentMin 15 --outFilterMismatchNmax 5. Aligned reads were deduplicated with UMI-tools (version 1.0.1) using --spliced-is-unique --per-cell --read-length --no-sort-output, and sorted using sambamba (version 0.7.1). Finally, aligned and deduplicated reads were parsed to extract the mapping coordinates and the reference sequence contexts around both the 5’ and 3’ ends of the reads. Count tables report the number of unique RPFs aligning within any annotated coding sequence for each gene. Reads were only counted if they aligned to the correct strand, if the trimmed length was between 30 and 45 nt long, and if the trimmed length was equal to the mapped length. Additionally, for these counts, the coding sequence was expanded to include 25 nt upstream and downstream of the start and stop codons, respectively. A random forest model was trained to predict the A-site location within an RPF read based on the footprint length and the sequence context around the 5’ and 3’ ends. The model was implemented in R (version 3.6.3) using ranger (version 0.12.1) with mlr (version 2.17.1) wrappers for training, tuning, assessment, and prediction. The model was trained on reads spanning a stop codon that satisfied the counting requirements listed above. The number of nucleotides between the 5’ end of these reads and the annotated stop codon was used for training.  VASA: Basecalls were performed on the Illumina NextSeq 2000. Reads from each plate were demultiplexed using bcl2fastq (version 2.20.0.422). Read 1 contains the UMI (fist 6 cycles), barcode (next 8 cycles), and a poly(T) stretch. Read 2 contains the insert. The UMI (first 6 cycles) and barcode (next 8 cycles) were extracted from read 1 and added to the read name. Adapter sequences were then trimmed from read 2 using cutadapt (version 2.7) with -U 6 -U -1 --times 5 -m :15  -A GTTCAGAGTTCTACA -A AAAAAAAAAAAAAAA -A TTTTTTTTTTTTTTT -A CCCCCCCCCCCCCCC -A GGGGGGGGGGGGGGG. Trimmed reads were aligned to the reference genome using STARSolo (version 2.7.3a_2020-05-22) with a 50-base overhang (--sjdbOverhang 50) with the following parameters: --seedSearchStartLmax 10 –alignIntronMax 1000000 --outFilterScoreMin 0 --outFilterMultimapNmax 1 --chimScoreSeparation 10 --chimScoreMin 20 --chimSegmentMin 15 --outFilterMismatchNmax 5. Aligned reads were deduplicated with UMI-tools (version 1.0.1) using --spliced-is-unique --per-cell –per-gene --read-length --no-sort-output, and sorted using sambamba (version 0.7.1). Finally, aligned and deduplicated reads were parsed to extract the mapping coordinates and the reference sequence contexts around both the 5’ and 3’ ends of the reads. Count tables report the number of unique reads aligning within any annotated transcript for each gene. Reads were only counted if they aligned to the correct strand.

!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = RPF: To generate ribosome-protected footprints, 2.5 µL of Micrococcal Nuclease (MNase, 62.5 U/µL, New England Biolabs) was added to each sample and incubated at 37 °C for 30 minutes. In order to stop digestion, 2.5 µL of stop mix [0.0186 U/µL Thermolabile Proteinase K (New England Biolabs), 62 mM EGTA (Sigma Aldrich), 16.5 mM EDTA (Ambion), and 697.5 mM guanidium thiocyanite (GuSCN, Sigma Aldrich)] was added to each well, and samples were incubated at 37 °C for 30 minutes then 55 °C for 10 minutes and held at 4 °C.  After ribosome footprint digestion, libraries were constructed using a one-pot small-RNA library preparation protocol that incorporated end repair, two RNA ligations, cDNA synthesis, and an indexing PCR. First, 2.5 µL of end-repair mix [4.1× of 10× T4 RNA Ligase Buffer (New England Biolabs), 16.4 mM MgCl2, 4.1 mM uridine triphosphate (New England Biolabs), 1.37 U/µL T4  Polynucleotide Kinase (New England Biolabs), and 0.82 U/µL RNaseIN Plus] was added to each well, and plates were incubated at 37 °C for 1 hour and held at 4 °C. Next, 13.2 µL of 3’ ligation brew [1× T4 RNA Ligase Buffer (New England Biolabs), 1 µM pre-adenylated 3’ adapter (OMV630 Integrated DNA Technologies), 35.5 % PEG-8000 (New England Biolabs), 0.1 % Tween-20 (Sigma Aldrich), 1 U/µL RNaseIN Plus, and 21.3 U/µL T4 RNA Ligase 2 Truncated KQ (New England Biolabs)] was added to each well and plates were incubated at 4 °C for 18 hours. The cDNA synthesis primer was then pre-annealed to the 3’ ligation products by adding 2.5 µL of the RT primer mix [5.2 µM RT primer (OMV572, Integrated DNA Technologies), 13.5 µM adenosine triphosphate (ATP, New England Biolabs), and 1 % Tween-20] to each well, heating to 65 °C for 1 minute, 37 °C for 2 minutes, 25 °C for 2 minutes, and holding at 4 °C. Five-prime adapters were then ligated by adding 7.8 µL of 5’ ligation brew [1× T4 RNA Ligase Buffer, 30.75 % PEG-8000, 0.1 % Tween-20, 0.5 µM 5’ adapter (OMV632, Integrated DNA Technologies), 1.25 U/µL T4 RNA Ligase 1 (Ambion)] and incubating at 37 °C for 2 hours and holding at 4 °C. Complementary DNA synthesis was then peformed by adding 38.5 µL of reverse transcription brew [1.88× 5× RT Buffer (ThermoFisher Scientific), 1.25 mM dNTPs (Promega), 0.1875 % Tween-20, 1.875 U/µL RNaseIN Plus, and 9.375 U/µL Maxima H Minus Reverse Transcriptase (ThermoFisher Scientific)] to each well, and heating at 50 °C for 1 hour, then 85 °C for 5 minutes and holding at 4 °C. Finally, libraries were indexed during PCR by first transferring 3.6 µL of 20 µM unique forward index primers (Integrated DNA Technologies) and 78 µL of PCR brew [1.5× Q5 Hot Start High-Fidelity 2× Master Mix (New England Biolabs), 0.15 % Tween-20, and 0.94 µM reverse index primer (Integrated DNA Technologies)] to half of the cDNA from each sample. Plates were then incubated at 98 °C for 30 s followed by 8 cycles of 98 °C for 15 s, 65 °C 30 s, 72 °C for 30 s, and then a final incubation at 72 °C for 5 min and holding at 4 °C. Plates were then frozen at -20 °C until gel purification.  After library construction the samples were purified. Product was cleaned up using AMPure XP beads (Beckman Coulter) that had been diluted 6× in bead binding buffer [20 % PEG-8000 (Sigma Aldrich) 2.5 M Sodium Chloride (Sigma Aldrich)]; diluted beads were added to the sample at a 2.1:1 ratio, and the final product was resuspended in 20 µL of low TE buffer [LoTET, 3 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0 (Ambion), 0.2 mM EDTA pH 8.0 (Gibco), 0.1 % Tween-20]. Half of each of the cleaned-up library pools was then run on a 20 cm 7 % polyacrylamide gel at 300 V until the lower marker ran off of the gel. The ~10 base-pair region from 175-185, corresponding to an insert size of ~30-40 nt was excised. The band was then crushed and soaked overnight at 4 °C in LTET8. Finally, eluate was cleaned up using AMPure XP beads that had been diluted 6× in bead binding buffer and resuspended in LTET8.  VASA: Cells were first lysed by adding 2.5 µL of Lysis and Fragmentation brew [3.4× First Strand Buffer (Invitrogen), 0.024 U/µL Thermolabile Proteinase K (New England Biolabs), 0.2 % Triton X-100, 1:125000 diluted ERCC Spike-in] and incubating at 25 °C for 1 hour, then 55 °C for 10 minutes and holding at 4 °C. Next, 2.5  µL of End Repair and Poly(A) tailing brew was added [0.6× First Strand Buffer (Invitrogen), 20 mM DTT (Invitrogen), 7.5 µM ATP (New England Biolabs), 0.075 U/µL E. coli Poly(A) Polymerase (New England Biolabs), 1 U/µL T4 Polynucleotide Kinase (New England Biolabs), 4 U/µL RNase OUT (Invitrogen)] and incubated at 37 °C for 1 hour and held at 4 °C. Then 2.5  µL of Reverse Transcription Brew was added [2 mM each dNTP (Promega), 16 U/µL SuperScript III (Invitrogen)] and incubated at 50 °C for 1 hour and held at 4 °C. Finally, 55 µL of Second Strand brew [1.14× Second Strand Buffer (Invitrogen), 0.23 mM each dNTP (Promega), 0.32 U/µL E. coli DNA Polymerase I (Invitrogen), 0.018 U/µL RNase H (Invitrogen)] was added and samples were incubated at 16 °C, 85 °C for 20 minutes, and held at 4 °C. Double stranded libraries were cleaned up using AMPure XP beads (Beckman) that had been diluted 8× in Bead Binding Buffer [20 % PEG-8000 (Sigma Aldrich), 2.5 M NaCl (Ambion), 0.02 % Sodium Azide (Sigma Aldrich)], resuspended in 12 µL of water, and amplified using in vitro transcription at 37 °C overnight using the MegaScript T7 kit (Invitrogen) in a 30 µL reaction.  In vitro transcription was stopped by adding 12 µL of ExoSAP-IT (Invitrogen) and incubating at 37 °C  for 15 minutes and holding at 4 °C. Resulting amplified RNA (aRNA) was then cleaned up using AMPure XP Beads at a 1.8× ratio and resuspended in 10 µL of water. 600 ng of aRNA was then mixed with 10 µL hybridization brew [100 mM Tris pH 7.5, 200 mM NaCl (Ambion), 5 µM rRNA depletion oligonucleotides], incubated at 95 °C for 2 minutes, then decreased to 45 °C at a rate of 0.1 °C/s. While keeping samples at 45 °C, 10 µL of RNase H mix [100 mM Tris pH 7.5, 200 mM NaCl (Ambion), 40 mM MgCl2, 1 U/µL Hybridase Thermostable RNase H (Epicenter)] was added, mixed by pipetting, and incubated at 45 °C for 30 minutes and held at 4 °C. Next, 30 µL of DNase mix [1.67 mM CaCl2, 0.133 U/µL RQ1 DNase (Promega)] was added and samples were incubated at 37 °C for 30 minutes. The resulting rRNA-depleted samples were cleaned up using AMPure XP beads at a 1.6× ratio and eluted in 5 µL of water.  The cleaned up, rRNA-depleted samples were then each mixed with 1 µL of RA3 adapter (/5rApp/TGGAATTCTCGGGTGCCAAGG/3ddC/, 20 µM), denatured at 70 °C for 2 minutes, then snap chilled on ice. 4 µL of ligation brew was added [2.5× T4 RNA Ligase Reaction Buffer (New England Biolabs), 50 U/µL T4 RNA Ligase 2 truncated (New England Biolabs), 10 U/µL RNaseOUT (Invitrogen), 12.5% PEG-8000 (New England Biolabs)], and incubated at 25 °C for 1 hour and held at 4 °C. Ligated RNA was then converted to cDNA by adding 3 µL of dNTP-primer mix [3.33 µM each dNTPs (Promega), 13.33 µM RTP (CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGA)], incubating at 65 °C for 5 minutes and snap chilling on ice. 8 µL of RT brew [2.5× First Strand Buffer (Invitrogen), 12.5 mM DTT (Invitrogen), 5 U/µL RNaseOUT (Invitrogen), 25 U/µL SuperScript III (Invitrogen)], was added, incubated at 50 °C for 1 hour, 70 °C for 15 minutes, and held at 4 °C. RNA was next degraded by adding 1 µL RNase A (10 mg/mL, ThermoFisher Scientific) and incubating at 37 °C for 30 minutes. Complementary DNA was cleaned up using AMPure XP beads at a 1× ratio and resuspended in 10 µL of water. Purified cDNA was amplified by adding PCR brew [1.25 × NEBNext PCR mix (New England Biolabs), 0.5 µM RP1 (AATGATACGGCGACCACCGAGATCTACACGTTCAGAGTTCTACAGTCCGA), and 0.5 µM indexed RPI (CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGAT[6 bases]GTGACTGGAGTTCCTTGGCACCCGAGAATTCCA)], and incubating at 98 °C for 30 s followed by 8 cycles of 98 °C for 15 s, 60 °C 30 s, 72 °C for 30 s, and then a final incubation at 72 °C for 5 min and holding at 4 °C. Amplified and indexed libraries were cleaned up with two successive AMPure XP bead cleanups with 0.8× ratio and resuspended in LTET8.

!Sample_data_processing = See github repository https://github.com/mvanins/scRiboSeq_manuscript/tree/main/data_processing for code associated with raw data processing. See github repository https://github.com/mvanins/WEE1_manuscript for scripts used to create associated figures.  RPF: Basecalls were performed on the Illumina NextSeq 2000. Reads from each plate were demultiplexed using bcl2fastq (version 2.20.0.422) with --use-bases-mask Y*,I*,Y* --no-lane-splitting --mask-short-adapter-reads 0 --minimum-trimmed-read-length 0. Read 1 contains the insert and Read 2 contains the cell barcode. Next, the UMI was extracted from the first 10 bases of read 1 and concatenated to the start of the cell barcode. Adapter sequences were then trimmed from read 1 using cutadapt (version 2.7) with -m 15: -a TGGAATTCTCGGGT. Trimmed reads were aligned to the reference genome using STARSolo (version 2.7.3a_2020-05-22) with a 50-base overhang (--sjdbOverhang 50) with the following parameters: --seedSearchStartLmax 10 –alignIntronMax 1000000 --outFilterScoreMin 0 --outFilterMultimapNmax 1 --chimScoreSeparation 10 --chimScoreMin 20 --chimSegmentMin 15 --outFilterMismatchNmax 5. Aligned reads were deduplicated with UMI-tools (version 1.0.1) using --spliced-is-unique --per-cell --read-length --no-sort-output, and sorted using sambamba (version 0.7.1). Finally, aligned and deduplicated reads were parsed to extract the mapping coordinates and the reference sequence contexts around both the 5’ and 3’ ends of the reads. Count tables report the number of unique RPFs aligning within any annotated coding sequence for each gene. Reads were only counted if they aligned to the correct strand, if the trimmed length was between 30 and 45 nt long, and if the trimmed length was equal to the mapped length. Additionally, for these counts, the coding sequence was expanded to include 25 nt upstream and downstream of the start and stop codons, respectively. A random forest model was trained to predict the A-site location within an RPF read based on the footprint length and the sequence context around the 5’ and 3’ ends. The model was implemented in R (version 3.6.3) using ranger (version 0.12.1) with mlr (version 2.17.1) wrappers for training, tuning, assessment, and prediction. The model was trained on reads spanning a stop codon that satisfied the counting requirements listed above. The number of nucleotides between the 5’ end of these reads and the annotated stop codon was used for training.  VASA: Basecalls were performed on the Illumina NextSeq 2000. Reads from each plate were demultiplexed using bcl2fastq (version 2.20.0.422). Read 1 contains the UMI (fist 6 cycles), barcode (next 8 cycles), and a poly(T) stretch. Read 2 contains the insert. The UMI (first 6 cycles) and barcode (next 8 cycles) were extracted from read 1 and added to the read name. Adapter sequences were then trimmed from read 2 using cutadapt (version 2.7) with -U 6 -U -1 --times 5 -m :15  -A GTTCAGAGTTCTACA -A AAAAAAAAAAAAAAA -A TTTTTTTTTTTTTTT -A CCCCCCCCCCCCCCC -A GGGGGGGGGGGGGGG. Trimmed reads were aligned to the reference genome using STARSolo (version 2.7.3a_2020-05-22) with a 50-base overhang (--sjdbOverhang 50) with the following parameters: --seedSearchStartLmax 10 –alignIntronMax 1000000 --outFilterScoreMin 0 --outFilterMultimapNmax 1 --chimScoreSeparation 10 --chimScoreMin 20 --chimSegmentMin 15 --outFilterMismatchNmax 5. Aligned reads were deduplicated with UMI-tools (version 1.0.1) using --spliced-is-unique --per-cell –per-gene --read-length --no-sort-output, and sorted using sambamba (version 0.7.1). Finally, aligned and deduplicated reads were parsed to extract the mapping coordinates and the reference sequence contexts around both the 5’ and 3’ ends of the reads. Count tables report the number of unique reads aligning within any annotated transcript for each gene. Reads were only counted if they aligned to the correct strand.
